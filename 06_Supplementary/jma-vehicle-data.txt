TOYOTA HIGHLANDER
================================================================================

What is the starting MSRP of the Toyota Highlander?
The starting MSRP of the Toyota Highlander is $38,945.

How many passengers does the Toyota Highlander seat?
The Toyota Highlander seats up to 8 passengers.

What is the engine in the Toyota Highlander?
The Toyota Highlander has a 2.4L Turbocharged 4-cylinder engine with 265 hp. A hybrid option is also available.

What is the fuel economy of the Toyota Highlander?
The Toyota Highlander gets 21 city / 29 highway (gas engine) or 36 city / 35 highway (hybrid).

What is the cargo space of the Toyota Highlander?
The Toyota Highlander has 16 cubic feet behind the 3rd row and 48.4 cubic feet behind the 2nd row.

What is the warranty of the Toyota Highlander?
The Toyota Highlander warranty includes:
- Basic warranty: 3 years / 36,000 miles
- Powertrain warranty: 5 years / 60,000 miles

What safety features does the Toyota Highlander have?
The Toyota Highlander comes standard with Toyota Safety Sense 3.0 on all trims.

================================================================================
TOYOTA SIENNA
================================================================================

What is the starting MSRP of the Toyota Sienna?
The starting MSRP of the Toyota Sienna is $37,185.

How many passengers does the Toyota Sienna seat?
The Toyota Sienna seats 7 or 8 passengers.

What is the engine in the Toyota Sienna?
The Toyota Sienna uses a 2.5L 4-cylinder Hybrid engine with 245 hp combined. All Sienna trims are hybrid only.

What is the fuel economy of the Toyota Sienna?
The Toyota Sienna gets 36 city / 36 highway mpg. All trims are hybrid.

What is the cargo space of the Toyota Sienna?
The Toyota Sienna has 33.5 cubic feet of cargo space behind the 3rd row.

What is the warranty of the Toyota Sienna?
The Toyota Sienna warranty includes:
- Basic warranty: 3 years / 36,000 miles
- Powertrain warranty: 5 years / 60,000 miles

What special features does the Toyota Sienna have?
The Toyota Sienna has available AWD on its hybrid system and sliding rear doors.

================================================================================
TOYOTA 4RUNNER
================================================================================

What is the starting MSRP of the Toyota 4Runner?
The starting MSRP of the Toyota 4Runner is $40,870.

How many passengers does the Toyota 4Runner seat?
The Toyota 4Runner seats up to 7 passengers (5 on the base trim).

What is the engine in the Toyota 4Runner?
The Toyota 4Runner has a 2.4L Turbocharged 4-cylinder engine with 278 hp (2025 redesign).

What is the fuel economy of the Toyota 4Runner?
The Toyota 4Runner gets 19 city / 24 highway mpg.

What is the cargo space of the Toyota 4Runner?
The Toyota 4Runner has 47.2 cubic feet of cargo space behind the 2nd row.

What is the warranty of the Toyota 4Runner?
The Toyota 4Runner warranty includes:
- Basic warranty: 3 years / 36,000 miles
- Powertrain warranty: 5 years / 60,000 miles

What special features does the Toyota 4Runner have?
The Toyota 4Runner is body-on-frame, off-road capable, and available with 4WD.

================================================================================
TOYOTA CAMRY
================================================================================

What is the starting MSRP of the Toyota Camry?
The starting MSRP of the Toyota Camry is $28,400.

How many passengers does the Toyota Camry seat?
The Toyota Camry seats 5 passengers.

What is the engine in the Toyota Camry?
The Toyota Camry uses a 2.5L 4-cylinder Hybrid engine with 225 hp. From 2025, the Camry is hybrid only.

What is the fuel economy of the Toyota Camry?
The Toyota Camry gets 51 city / 53 highway mpg.

What is the warranty of the Toyota Camry?
The Toyota Camry warranty includes:
- Basic warranty: 3 years / 36,000 miles
- Powertrain warranty: 5 years / 60,000 miles

What special features does the Toyota Camry have?
From 2025, all Toyota Camry hybrid trims come standard with AWD.

================================================================================
WARRANTY POLICY - ALL TOYOTA VEHICLES
================================================================================

What is ToyotaCare?
ToyotaCare is included with all Toyota vehicles. It provides 2 years or 25,000 miles of free maintenance.

Is an extended warranty available?
Yes. Toyota Extra Care Gold extended warranty is available for up to 10 years or 125,000 miles.

What does the powertrain warranty cover?
The Toyota powertrain warranty covers the engine, transmission, and drivetrain.

What is the corrosion warranty?
Toyota offers a corrosion warranty of 5 years with unlimited miles.
