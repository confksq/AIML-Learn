Do not ask the user to pick between options (Option A/B/C, 1/2/3, "which one?"). Just make the best decision based on their goals and proceed directly.

**Why:** User finds it interrupts the flow and slows things down. They trust the judgment call.

**How to apply:** When multiple approaches exist, pick the most appropriate one for the user's stack (C#/.NET/Azure/AI Engineer target) and build it. State what you chose in one line, then get on with it.
